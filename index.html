FAQ

Questions, answered

Is my data private?

Yes. On the free plan everything is stored locally in your own browser — nothing is uploaded. Cloud sync on paid plans is encrypted and only visible to you.

What kinds of links can it detect?

Instagram reels & posts, TikTok, YouTube (including Shorts), Vimeo, PDFs, Google Docs/Sheets/Slides, Drive files, Office documents, image links, and plain-text notes. Anything else lands in a general Links shelf.

Do I need an account to try it?

Nope. Scroll up, drop a file or paste a link, and it works immediately. Create an account later only if you want sync across devices.

Can I save Instagram reels and videos?

Absolutely — that's a core use case. Paste a reel link and Dump files it under Reels with a one-tap "Open" back to the original.

Will it work on my phone?

Yes. Dump is fully responsive and works in any modern mobile browser.
